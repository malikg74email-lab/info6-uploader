Privacy Policy — Info 6 Uploader

Info 6 Uploader is a personal, single-user application operated by its owner, Azhar Farooq, for one purpose only: uploading the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (Info 6) using the YouTube Data API.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's Google authorization, the app uses the YouTube upload scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) to upload video files and set their titles, descriptions and tags on the owner's channel.

Data storage

The app stores its OAuth token only on the owner's own computer. Video files remain the owner's files. The app runs no servers, collects no analytics, and keeps no database of user information.

Data sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with any third party. Data is sent only to Google's YouTube API as required to perform the upload the owner requested.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops the app from acting on the account.
